A little about me

I care about how a game feels as much as how it works.

I'm building my craft by making playable projects from the ground up, testing mechanics, shaping atmosphere, and learning how small design decisions change the player's experience. I like exploring different genres rather than locking myself into just one.

This portfolio documents the work, the experiments, and the progress. More projects will find their way here as they become ready to share.

How I work

Prototype. Play. Refine.

I believe in building small, testing early, and polishing the details that make a game feel intentional.

01 / EXPLORE

Find the hook

Start with a clear mechanic or feeling that makes the experience worth trying.

02 / BUILD

Make it playable

Turn the idea into a working prototype, then test whether the core loop actually lands.

03 / POLISH

Make it memorable

Refine feedback, sound, atmosphere, and presentation so the whole experience feels cohesive.
